# Manual advancement

Open **Journal & context → Advancement → Review advancement**. The selected provider reviews completed, non-undone, finalized turns not covered by an applied review. Previously reviewed raw conversations are omitted; the request includes the previous cumulative story summary, unfinished objectives, progression policy and award ledger. Large backlogs run in sequential batches with durable checkpoints. Successful batches survive interruptions; no turns become covered until Apply.

Rewards follow the campaign's game system and edition. Combat, objectives, failures, roleplay and other events can qualify when that system supports them. Published rule originals take precedence over model knowledge; campaign references can establish house rules. The dedicated provider receives isolated reference lookup tools, with saved original-text receipts, and no gameplay or sheet mutation tools. There are no sessions: a click never counts as a session. Session-based rules receive an explained continuous-play house rule, retained for future reviews. Ambiguous systems or prior explicit rewards can produce a request for guidance instead of invented rewards.

The result is a proposal. Edit award amounts and reasons directly, or use **Edit full proposal** to change system identity, units, recipients, evidence and policy. Enter an adjustment reason and **Save proposal edits**; this makes no additional AI call. **Apply awards and mark turns reviewed** records the saved final proposal and covers every captured turn, including a zero-award review. Discard, cancellation, failure and staging consume no turns. Changed instructions, selected rules, supplied references, prior ledger or captured gameplay require a fresh review. Unrelated sheet edits and newer gameplay do not invalidate an otherwise valid proposal.

This feature is a ledger only. It never updates character attributes, inventory, descriptions, notes, levels, XP balances or spending. After Apply, update your character sheet manually. Player sheets show one read-only **Awarded through this feature** field, calculated from all applied final awards since this feature began. Existing sheet XP and later spending do not affect it. Different game editions and reward currencies remain separate in the same display. Milestones and improvement eligibility are historical counts, not invented numeric XP.

**Reverse** operates newest applied review first, retaining the audit but removing its coverage and totals. The preceding review becomes the current summary/policy. Reconcile your sheet manually after reversal. Gameplay undo refuses to undo a covered turn until its covering reviews have been reversed. Applied and reversed awards, names of deleted recipients, coverage, proposals and reference receipts survive campaign export/import with remapped identities. Imported pending proposals are audit-only and cannot execute. Campaign templates carry no play-derived reviews or award history.

Migration `0021_manual_advancement.sql` creates review, coverage, decision-replay and reference-receipt tables. Apply migrations with `npm run migrate`, build with `npm run build`, and restart the backend. Generation remains cancellable without an application-imposed AI deadline. Local provider prompt traces use purpose `advancement_review`; logs and campaign exports can contain private campaign material.
